Your website, scored honestly

A full review of justinaford.com covering the technical build, how search engines see it, how it compares to the coaches ranking around you, and how well the site is set up to bring you clients. Every area below is rated out of 10, and every number was measured directly from your live site on 18 August 2026.

3.1 OUT OF 10

Strong content sitting on a foundation that is working against it

The writing, the credentials and the podcast are genuinely good, and they are the hardest part of this to build. What is holding the site back is almost entirely mechanical: it measures nothing, it is slow and unstable on phones, and it hides most of its own pages from Google. None of the ten areas below scored badly because of your content. The clearest evidence is in the search data: your site currently ranks for 24 keywords in total, none of them in the top three, and it does not appear in the top 20 for a single one of the terms a client would type to find a coach.

What we would do next

In this order

Sequenced by what each step depends on, not by how quick it is.

Turn measurement on and secure the basics

Connect analytics so every change after this can be judged on results rather than opinion, fix the email records so your list reaches the inbox, and close the file and account exposure.

Make the site fast and stable on a phone

Address the image weight and the layout jumping, which together account for the two worst scores in this report and affect every visitor you have.

Reconnect the site to itself and decide what stays

Bring the orphaned pages, the contact page and the podcast page back into the structure, and settle what happens to the 187 pages from the beauty agency era.

Pick the ground you can win and claim your authority

Settle how you want to handle a name that Google gives to someone else, build on the openings where your credentials and the search demand already line up, make your podcast and profiles readable by search engines, then re-run this exact report at 30, 60 and 90 days to show movement.
